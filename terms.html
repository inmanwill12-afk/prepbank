Terms, Privacy & Refunds

PrepBank is a practice-test website for students at Highland Park High School in Dallas, Texas. It is run independently by a student and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Highland Park ISD.

The short version
  • Free accounts can take every free test and share their own study material.
  • PrepBank+ costs $3.00 per month and unlocks every test in every class.
  • Cancel anytime in Settings → Billing. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for. We don't give refunds for partial months.
  • We never see or store your card number. Stripe handles all payments.
  • Questions? Email inmanwill12@yahoo.com.

Terms of use

By creating an account you agree to these terms. If you are under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian is okay with you using PrepBank.

  • Study honestly. PrepBank is for practice. Don't upload real tests, quizzes or answer keys your teacher hasn't handed out to study from, and follow your school's academic honesty rules.
  • Share only what you're allowed to share. Upload study guides, notes and review sheets. Don't upload anything that isn't yours to share.
  • Keep it school-appropriate. Tests and display names containing inappropriate language are blocked automatically. We may remove content or accounts that break these rules.
  • AI-generated content can be wrong. Practice questions are written by AI from material students share and may contain mistakes. Check anything important against your notes and your teacher. PrepBank isn't responsible for grades.
  • Your content. You keep ownership of what you upload. By publishing a test you let other PrepBank users practice with it. You can delete your tests from My tests at any time.
  • PrepBank is provided "as is." We may change or discontinue features, and we'll update this page if these terms change.

PrepBank+ billing & refunds

  • Price: $3.00 USD per month, charged to your card when you subscribe and again every month until you cancel.
  • Who pays: If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must make or approve the purchase.
  • Cancel anytime: Go to Settings → Billing → Manage billing or cancel. Cancelling stops future charges, and you keep PrepBank+ until the end of the current paid month.
  • Refunds: Payments are non-refundable, including partial months. If you were charged by mistake or charged twice, email us and we'll fix it.
  • Payments are processed securely by Stripe.

Privacy policy

What we collect:

  • Your email address, display name and profile picture (if you add one).
  • Tests you create, and your practice results so you can see your scores and so authors can earn leaderboard points.
  • Your theme and color choices, and the classes and tests you pin.
  • If you subscribe: your subscription status from Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never reach PrepBank.

How it's used: only to run PrepBank. When you create a test, the study material you share is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to write the questions. Your display name, profile picture and leaderboard points are visible to other signed-in students.

Who we share it with: only the services that run the site: Supabase (accounts and database), Vercel (hosting), Stripe (payments) and Anthropic (AI question writing). We never sell your information or show ads.

Deleting your data: email us from your account's email address and we'll delete your account and everything in it.
